About Almora

Almora is a lively Kumaoni town famous for its handicrafts, sunset points, and old-world charm. Its markets and temples reflect a rich hill culture and scenic mountain scenery.

Highlights

Bright End Corner, Katarmal Sun Temple, local crafts, quiet walking lanes.

Best Time to Visit

March to June and October to November for clear views and cool weather.

How to Reach

By road from Ranikhet, Nainital, or Kathgodam.
