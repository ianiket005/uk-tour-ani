About Auli

Auli is renowned for its skiing slopes, cable cars, and majestic views of Nanda Devi. It’s a winter sports paradise and a beautiful summer retreat for scenic walks.

Highlights

Auli ski slopes, ropeway ride, Nanda Devi views, Gurso Bugyal meadows, Kuri Village.

Best Time to Visit

November to February for skiing; April to June for pleasant weather and wildflowers.

How to Reach

Drive from Joshimath (16 km) after reaching Rishikesh or Haridwar by road.
