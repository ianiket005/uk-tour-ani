About Champawat

Champawat is an ancient town with archaeological sites, historic temples, and lovely valley scenery. It is a peaceful stop for those exploring eastern Kumaon.

Highlights

Baleshwar Temple, Anand Acharya Ashram, terraced fields, rugged hill trails.

Best Time to Visit

March to June and September to November for clear weather.

How to Reach

Accessible by road from Pithoragarh or Almora.

Top Attractions

  • Visit Baleshwar Temple
  • Explore the old market
  • Walk the terraced fields
  • Relax at local ashrams and viewpoints

Travel Tips

  • Hire a local guide for temple history tours.
  • Carry water and sun protection for hill walks.
  • Enjoy simple local foods in the town market.
