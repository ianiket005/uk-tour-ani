About Chaukori

Chaukori is famous for tea gardens and sunrise viewpoints overlooking the Panchachuli peaks. It is a lesser-known destination for travelers seeking calm and mountain charm.

Highlights

Tea gardens, sunrise points, trekking, panoramic Himalayan views.

Best Time to Visit

March to June and September to November for clear mountain vistas.

How to Reach

Road access from Pithoragarh or Almora via quiet hill roads.

Top Attractions

  • Watch sunrise over Panchachuli peaks
  • Walk through tea plantations
  • Visit quiet hamlets and viewpoints
  • Enjoy local Kumaoni tea and snacks

Travel Tips

  • Wake early for the sunrise viewpoint.
  • Carry a light jacket for cool mornings.
  • Book homestays close to the tea gardens.
