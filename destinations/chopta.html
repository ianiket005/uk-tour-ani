About Chopta

Chopta is a quiet retreat surrounded by pine forests and snow-capped peaks. It serves as the base for the popular Tungnath temple trek and offers beautiful mountain scenery year-round.

Highlights

Tungnath and Chandrashila treks, Deoria Tal, panoramic Himalayan views, wildflower meadows.

Best Time to Visit

March to June and September to November for clear skies and pleasant hikes.

How to Reach

Road access from Rishikesh via Rudraprayag and Ukhimath; the last stretch is through hilly roads.
