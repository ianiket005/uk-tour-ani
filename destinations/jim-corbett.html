About Jim Corbett

Jim Corbett National Park stretches across forested valleys and grasslands, offering wildlife safaris and nature stays. It’s ideal for spotting tigers, elephants, deer, and bird species.

Highlights

Jeep safaris, elephant rides, birdwatching, Corbett Museum, Garjiya Devi Temple.

Best Time to Visit

November to June when the park is open and wildlife sightings are best.

How to Reach

Nearest railhead is Ramnagar, followed by a short taxi ride into the park.

Top Attractions

  • Morning jeep safari for tigers
  • Visit Dhikala or Bijrani zones
  • Go birdwatching at sunrise
  • Explore the Corbett Museum
  • Walk to the scenic Garjiya Devi Temple

Travel Tips

  • Book safari permits early in peak season.
  • Carry binoculars and insect repellent.
  • Respect park rules and keep noise to a minimum.
