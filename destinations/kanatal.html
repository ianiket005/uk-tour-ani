About Kanatal

Kanatal offers solitude and scenic beauty above Mussoorie, with pine forests, apple orchards, and a relaxed village feel. It is ideal for nature walks and small group retreats.

Highlights

Surkanda Devi trek, Tehri Lake views, jungle stays, quiet forest trails.

Best Time to Visit

March to June and September to November for crisp weather and clear skies.

How to Reach

Accessible by road from Dehradun via Mussoorie and Dhanaulti.
