About Mukteshwar

Mukteshwar sits on a cliff overlooking the Kumaon Himalayas. It is famous for long walks, birdwatching, and relaxed forest stays.

Highlights

Mukteshwar Temple, Chauli Ki Jali, local orchards, nature trails.

Best Time to Visit

March to June and September to November for the best skies and weather.

How to Reach

Drive from Nainital, Almora, or Kathgodam via hill roads.
