About Mukteshwar

Mukteshwar sits on a cliff overlooking the Kumaon Himalayas. It is famous for long walks, birdwatching, and relaxed forest stays.

Highlights

Mukteshwar Temple, Chauli Ki Jali, local orchards, nature trails.

Best Time to Visit

March to June and September to November for the best skies and weather.

How to Reach

Drive from Nainital, Almora, or Kathgodam via hill roads.

Top Attractions

  • See panoramic views from Chauli Ki Jali
  • Visit the old Mukteshwar Temple
  • Explore organic orchards
  • Walk along forest trails at sunrise

Travel Tips

  • Stay in a cliffside cottage for dramatic views.
  • Bring binoculars for birdwatching.
  • Avoid trekking after dark on narrow paths.
