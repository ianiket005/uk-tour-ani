About Naukuchiatal

Naukuchiatal is a lesser-known lake town with a beautifully shaped nine-cornered lake. It offers a quiet alternative to nearby Nainital, with birdwatching and serene waters.

Highlights

Boating, birdwatching, paragliding, lakeside walks, nature photography.

Best Time to Visit

March to June and September to November for mild weather and bird activity.

How to Reach

It is about 35 km from Kathgodam, followed by a short drive through hill roads.
