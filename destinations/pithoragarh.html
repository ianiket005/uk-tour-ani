About Pithoragarh

Pithoragarh provides dramatic vistas of the eastern Himalayas and access to remote valleys. It is known for its historic fort and vibrant local markets.

Highlights

Pithoragarh Fort, Kali River valley, Askot wildlife sanctuary, panoramic viewpoints.

Best Time to Visit

March to June and September to November for bright mountain views.

How to Reach

Road access from Almora, Tanakpur, and Kathgodam.
